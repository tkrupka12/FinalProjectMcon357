Help & Support Center

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a lesson plan?

Simply start chatting with the AI assistant! It will ask you about your grade level, subject, and topic. You can answer naturally, and the AI will create a personalized lesson plan for you.

Can I modify a lesson plan after it's created?

Yes! After the lesson plan is generated, you'll be asked if you want to add or change anything. Just tell the AI what you'd like to modify, and it will update the plan for you.

How do I save my lesson plans?

All your conversations and lesson plans are automatically saved. You can access them anytime from the sidebar on the left.

Can I export my lesson plans?

Yes! Once a lesson plan is generated, you can export it as a PDF or Word document using the export buttons.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all your data is stored securely and is only accessible to you. We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information.
